## Changed

- **The two-finger swipe up submits as in TalkBack's braille keyboard.** The keyboard now stays open after sending, so you can type the next message straight away; apps such as search boxes still close it when they are done. In a field that holds many lines and has no send action, you hear "No action available" instead of the keyboard closing.
- **English braille tables say which code they use.** The literary tables are now listed as English (UEB) and English (United States, EBAE). EBAE is the American braille used before 2016. If you write UEB, for example the at sign as dots 4 and 1, or numbers inside a word as in an email address, choose English (UEB), as EBAE reads these differently.
- **When touch pass-through has been turned off, the keyboard tells you.** Some phones, such as Xiaomi phones, turn it off when they close apps to save battery. Touching the keyboard now explains this, and double tapping it opens the accessibility settings at touch pass-through so you can turn it on again. The user guide has a new troubleshooting section on stopping this.

## Fixed

- In fields that hold many lines and have no send action, the two-finger swipe up closed the keyboard without sending anything.
- The action now follows your text by a moment, as in TalkBack, for apps such as Google Messages that check what there is to send just before sending.
- With circles hidden, TalkBack didn't say why the keyboard wasn't getting your touches.
