## New

- **The keyboard menu opens on screen with a screen reader.** Swipe up with three fingers and the menu opens as a dialog over the keyboard, which you explore with TalkBack or Jieshuo like any other. Closing it, or choosing an action such as Read all text, takes you back to the keyboard. Settings and the user guide open in their own screens. Without a screen reader the menu is spoken as before.
- **Change settings from the menu.** Settings in the menu say their value, for example "Auto-correct, Off". Double tap one to move to its next value. Its values are also listed in the screen reader's actions for it, and double tap and hold lists them to choose from. Braille type and braille table are now settings in the menu too.
- **The keyboard menu with the classic gestures.** Swiping up with three fingers now opens the menu with every gesture style.
- **Interactive tutorial.** Choose Start tutorial in the keyboard menu to learn typing and the main gestures of your gesture style one step at a time. Each step tells you what to do and, if you do something else, what the keyboard felt. Typing and gestures don't change your text while it runs. Stop it any time from the menu.

## Fixed

- With contracted braille, cells typed in the middle of a word that only stand for a contraction, such as "ch", "sh" or "ing", were silent or said the letters before them again. They are now said as the contraction. "In" and "en" are no longer said as 9 and 5.
