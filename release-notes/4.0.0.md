## New

- **Braille tables for 11 more languages.** Arabic, Catalan, Estonian, Hebrew, Hindi, Hungarian, Icelandic, Lithuanian, Norwegian (grades 0 to 3), Serbian and Slovenian, plus Danish grade 2, French grade 2 (France and Canada), German grade 0 and Swiss German, and English (Canada) and Russian computer braille. Every table was checked on a phone by typing real words in its language.
- **Speak through the screen reader.** A new option in Text To Speech Settings, off by default. When it is on and TalkBack, Jieshuo or another screen reader is running, the keyboard speaks with the screen reader's voice instead of its own. If the screen reader turns explore by touch off while the keyboard is open, as Jieshuo can, the keyboard speaks with its own voice.
- **Delete all text with the TalkBack gestures.** Swipe left with two fingers twice within half a second.

## Fixed

- After moving or reading by character or word, deleting now removes the text you just heard, not the character before it.
- With Jieshuo, touches on the keyboard no longer go to explore by touch every other time while touch pass-through is on.
