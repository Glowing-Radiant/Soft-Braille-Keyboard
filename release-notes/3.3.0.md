## New

- **Gesture practice.** While it is on, gestures say what they do and how they were recognised instead of doing it, for example "New line. swipe right with two fingers". Typing still works. Turn it on or off in the keyboard menu or the settings.
- **Auto-correct.** When you type a space after a misspelled word, it is replaced by the spell checker's suggestion and you hear "corrected to" and the word. Delete the space straight away to get your word back. Off by default; turn it on in the settings or the keyboard menu.
- **Sound themes.** Each kind of action can have its own sound: typing, space, delete, new line, send, moving, selecting, settings, the menu and errors. Choose Minimal, Abstract, Soft, Wood, Typewriter, Perkins, Bubble or Glass in Keyboard Feedback Settings, or import your own sounds.

## Changed

- The TalkBack new line gesture now adds a line break instead of pressing enter, so it never sends a message by accident. In a single line field you hear "Single line field" instead.

## Fixed

- Deleting a character or word while text is selected now deletes the selection, as other editors do.
