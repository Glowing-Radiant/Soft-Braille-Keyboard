## New

- **A built-in spell checker.** The keyboard now checks spelling itself with Hunspell, the spell checker of LibreOffice and Firefox, and comes with US and UK English dictionaries. Auto-correct, the misspelling echo and the spelling gestures use it, and suggest better corrections than the phone's spell checker, for example "receive" for "recieve". Languages without a dictionary still use the phone's spell checker. For English tables that don't name a country, the dictionary for your phone's country is used if there is one, otherwise UK English.
- **Spell checker settings.** In Miscellaneous Settings, choose between the built-in dictionaries and the phone's spell checker, import a Hunspell dictionary for another language (its .dic and .aff files, named after the language, such as de_DE.dic and de_DE.aff), and remove imported ones.

## Changed

- **Deleting works like backspace.** Deleting never removes text after the cursor. After moving or reading, the character you just heard is deleted too, as if the cursor were just after it. Before, the delete word gesture could delete the word after the cursor, and deleting again went on deleting what followed.

## Fixed

- A word could be announced as misspelled and then not be found by the spelling gestures, as they checked in different languages and by different rules. They now agree.
- Words the spell checker doesn't know but that don't look like typos, such as names, are no longer called misspelled by the spelling gestures.
